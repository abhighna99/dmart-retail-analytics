Critical Finding: Lack of Loyalty Tier Differentiation

In this dataset, Platinum members generate ₹773.51 in ARPU, which is actually slightly lower than Non-Members (₹779.06) and virtually identical to Silver members (₹771.17). This indicates that the current loyalty program offers zero tier-gated spending incentives or gamified rewards. DMart has an urgent strategic opportunity to revamp the loyalty tier structure by introducing minimum spend thresholds to qualify for Platinum, tier-exclusive discounts on fresh produce, and milestone vouchers.

Merchandising & Basket Value

Cross-Category Bundle Merchandising

Staples account for 30.9% of sales at 19.2% margin. Customers are purchasing staple items in isolation (1.51 UPT). DMart should implement physical and digital cross-merchandising bundles (e.g. "Buy 5kg Rice, get 20% off Biscuits/Chocolates" or "Breakfast Combo: Bread + Tea + Sugar").

Expected Business Impact: +12% ABV & +180 bps Margin
Customer Retention & LTV

Tier-Gated Loyalty Program Overhaul

Currently, Platinum members generate ₹773.51 in ARPU vs ₹779.06 for Non-Members. Re-architect the loyalty scheme with strict annual spending thresholds (e.g., ₹5,000 to reach Gold, ₹12,000 to reach Platinum), offering exclusive weekend grocery pricing, express checkout, and free home delivery.

Expected Business Impact: +25% Platinum ARPU
Operational Efficiency

Online Return Rate & Cancellation Mitigation

Online returns (3.51%) and cancellations (4.10%) are the highest among channels, causing ₹1.14L in lost revenue and ₹82.3k in returns. Prioritize Mobile App promotion (which has the lowest 2.97% return rate) via first-order app discounts, and implement pre-dispatch address verification and inventory sync.

Expected Business Impact: Save ₹50,000+ in annual waste
Data Governance & Engineering

Automated POS Validation Constraints

Enforce schema validation at POS ingestion: block discount inputs > 20% without store manager authorization override, enforce catalog price lookup rather than manual cashier price entry (preventing negative prices), and mandate standardized ISO-8601 date timestamps.

Expected Business Impact: Eliminates 100% data entry glitches
